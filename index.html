V Vaccination triage checker

Important: this is not medical advice

This list was produced by a design prototype, as a demonstration of how eligibility triage could work. It is not a real health service, it is not connected to any vaccination record, and nothing in it should be treated as medical advice.

It is based only on a handful of self-reported answers and a simplified version of the routine schedule. It may be incomplete, out of date, or wrong. Do not use it to decide whether to have, or not have, any vaccine.

Always speak to a GP, practice nurse, pharmacist or midwife before acting on anything listed here.

Rules are based on the published UK routine immunisation schedule and are held in an editable data layer, separate from the application logic. Dates and cohort boundaries need clinical sign-off before any real use.
